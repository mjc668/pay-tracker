## Notes

Roadmap P3 plus an account-level default currency. Four UI wins: a payments calendar (toggle) and dashboard mini-calendar; filters on the payments and bills lists; a six-month expected-spend forecast drawn on the dashboard trend chart; and a series generator that pre-creates future payment instances. Also: users get a `default_currency` setting used as the initial currency when creating a bill.
